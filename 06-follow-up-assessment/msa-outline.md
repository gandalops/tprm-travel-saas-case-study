# Master services agreement: blank outline

> A blank outline, headings only. It is a working list of clauses, not legal advice. Legal and Procurement draft the actual text. The required terms are tracked in `contract-checklist.xlsx` (CC-01 to CC-24).

- Parties and definitions
- Scope of services (service description, locations)
- Term and validity (start date, renewal, expiry)
- Fees and payment terms
- Service levels (SLA) and service credits
- Roles and responsibilities (bank and vendor)
- Data protection and privacy (processor terms, data location, transfers)
- Information security requirements
- Subcontracting and fourth parties (notice, objection, flow-down)
- Incident notification and assistance
- Business continuity and disaster recovery
- Right to audit (access, inspection, audit)
- Cooperation with regulators
- Right to exit (termination triggers, notice, transition period, data return and deletion, insolvency)
- Confidentiality and intellectual property
- Liability, indemnity and insurance
- Compliance with laws (sanctions, anti-bribery)
- Change management
- Governance and reporting (meetings, KPIs, reviews)
- Governing law, jurisdiction and dispute resolution
- Assignment and change of control
- Notices and contacts
- Amendments and order of precedence
- Annexes (service description, SLA, data processing terms, security schedule, subprocessor list, exit plan)
